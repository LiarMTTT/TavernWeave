## Scope limits

- The PNG check establishes protected replacement and repacking on that file. Its small edit leaves other inherited masculine pronouns outside the patch; it is not a completed semantic adaptation, a name-choice trial or a full portrayal-quality pass.
- The EPUB check establishes selected-text ingestion and shared fact promotion. It does not claim novel-card generation, review of a full chapter/novel or a new roleplay result. A spine item is not necessarily a complete literary chapter.
- The unchanged shared serializer and earlier source/build behavior retain their historical evidence. The 40-check 0.3.1 baseline was not rerun. New TXT chapter cataloging reuses the pinned upstream splitter; no duplicate whole-text converter or new general authoring engine was tested.
- Supported paths include legacy/v2/v3 JSON, standalone JSON worldbooks and PNG tEXt/zTXt/iTXt decoding, but this round is not exhaustive proof across every envelope, compression variant, corrupted file or host. The protected editor refuses schema remodeling and incompatible payload structures.
- Dedicated name-review behavior, arbitrary script edits, cross-dialect worldbook migration, complete component assembly and native host registration are not covered by these checks. Existing source-age and player-agency policies remain in force.

No live SillyTavern import, image generation, novel-card generation or roleplay was performed in this round. Earlier user-accepted trials remain separate evidence. Publication checks cover the package manifest, version/links, changed-path scope and the remote tree; they do not add a new source sample.
